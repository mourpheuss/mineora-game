MINEORA PROTOCOL ARCHITECTURE

Closed-Loop Production, Natural Reserve Anchor & Free Exchange

1. Value Standard (Peg Mechanism)

The ORA commodity extracted in MINEORA is mathematically anchored to 1 ORA = 0.10 USDT ($0.10) across the entire protocol. Value generation is driven exclusively by active extraction and operational leadership.

2. Four-Depth Referral Network

Track your lineage up to 4 full depths. Career license acquisitions distribute commissions according to an inverted model (Tier 1: 10%, Tier 2: 7%, Tier 3: 5%, Tier 4: 3%).

3. Free Market (P2P Exchange) & Escrow

Extracted ORA assets are traded directly between players for USDT via the P2P Exchange. The protocol acts as an impartial escrow agent ensuring safety and instant settlement.

4. Deflationary Burn Furnace

Ecosystem supply is capped. Protocol fees collected from elevator runs, stake yields, and P2P trades are redirected to the dEaD burn address, perpetually preserving scarcity.
